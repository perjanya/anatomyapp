Clinical Reasoning Questions

Reason through each finding before opening the answer.

After a fracture of the humeral shaft, a patient cannot extend the wrist. Which nerve is likely injured, and why?

The radial nerve runs in the radial groove of the humerus and supplies the wrist extensors.

After an anterior shoulder dislocation, a patient has difficulty abducting the arm beyond the initial phase and has reduced sensation over the lateral shoulder. Which nerve explains both findings?

The axillary nerve supplies the deltoid and gives rise to the superior lateral cutaneous nerve of the arm.

After a fall on an outstretched hand, tenderness is localised to the anatomical snuffbox. Which bone is commonly implicated?

The scaphoid. It forms part of the floor of the anatomical snuffbox.

A patient has difficulty spreading the fingers after an injury behind the medial epicondyle. Which nerve and muscles explain this finding?

The ulnar nerve passes behind the medial epicondyle and supplies the dorsal interossei, which abduct the fingers.
