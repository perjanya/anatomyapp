MCQs – Upper Limb

Choose an answer before revealing the explanation.

1. Which structure lies lateral to the brachial artery in the cubital fossa?

  1. Median nerve
  2. Biceps tendon
  3. Ulnar nerve
Reveal answer

B. The biceps tendon lies immediately lateral to the brachial artery.

2. Which muscle initiates abduction of the arm at the shoulder?

  1. Supraspinatus
  2. Latissimus dorsi
  3. Pectoralis major
Reveal answer

A. Supraspinatus assists initiation of abduction, and the deltoid becomes the principal abductor through the middle range.

3. Which nerve supplies the dorsal interossei of the hand?

  1. Median nerve
  2. Radial nerve
  3. Ulnar nerve
Reveal answer

C. The deep branch of the ulnar nerve supplies the dorsal interossei.

4. Which artery is the main contributor to the superficial palmar arch?

  1. Radial artery
  2. Ulnar artery
  3. Anterior interosseous artery
Reveal answer

B. The superficial palmar arch is predominantly supplied by the ulnar artery.
