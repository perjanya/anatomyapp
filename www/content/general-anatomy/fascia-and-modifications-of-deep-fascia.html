Fascia and modifications of deep fascia

Connective tissue large enough to be seen by unaided eyes is fascia

Fascia is divided into 

superficial fascia and deep fascia

Loose connective tissue that merges with deeper aspect of dermis is - Superficial fascia

Thickness of superficial fascia varies

Fascia between deeper structures is - Deep fascia

There are many modifications of deep fascia

Superficial fascia is absent in 

  • Eyelids
  • External ear / pinna
  • Penis
  • Scrotum

Modifications of Deep fascia

Retinaculum

  • Band around tendons that holds them in place

Eg: Flexor retinaculum of hand

Aponeurosis

  • Sheet like expansion of the tendinous insertion of the muscle

Eg: Bicipital aponeurosis of biceps brachii attaches tendon of the muscle to medial aspect of the forearm

Aponeurosis of frontalis and occipitalis muscles forms the third layer of scalp

Fibrous sheath

  • Connective tissue covering other structures

Eg: Carotid sheath covers great vessels of the neck

Fibrous capsules

  • Connective tissue covering organs and glands

Eg: parotid sheath covers parotid gland

Intermuscular septa

  • Connective tissue between the muscles

Eg: medial and lateral intermuscular septa divide arm into different compartments
